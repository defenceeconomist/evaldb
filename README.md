# defence-evaluation-database

The goal of the defence evaluation database is to compile a list of defence evaluations and key metadata.

## What is evaluation?

UK Government definition (Magenta Book, HM Treasury, 2020)

“Evaluation is the systematic assessment of the design, implementation and outcomes of an intervention. It is intended to provide information that is credible and useful, enabling the incorporation of lessons learned into decision-making processes.”

## Known Repositories of Defence Evaluations

Here’s a concise, **region-by-region index of public repositories/sources where defence evaluations (in the Magenta-style sense: systematic, evidence-based assessments of design/implementation/outcomes) and adjacent audit/OT\&E material are published.** 

### United Kingdom (NATO)

* **Evaluation Registry (cross-government, incl. MOD)** – mandatory from 1 April 2024 for planned/live/completed evaluations. ([GOV.UK][1])
* **MOD Evaluation Strategy + PDF** – governance and forward plan for MOD evaluations. ([GOV.UK][2])
* **NAO (MOD reports)** – VfM/effectiveness audits of the Equipment Plan and major capabilities (e.g., EP 2023–33; F-35). ([National Audit Office (NAO)][3])
* **Dstl/Athena** – repository/indices of MOD science & technical reports (often include evaluation of trials/experiments). ([GOV.UK][4])


### United States (NATO)

* **DOT\&E Annual Reports (OT\&E & LFT\&E)** – programme-by-programme operational test findings. ([Dote][5])
* **GAO (Weapon Systems Annual Assessment + topic pages)** – recurring cost/schedule/performance evaluations and acquisition governance. ([Government Accountability Office][6])
* **DTIC Public** – searchable repository of unclassified DoD technical/evaluation reports. ([discover.dtic.mil][7])
* **Selected Acquisition Reports (SARs) – OSD/CAPE (archival public sets)** – cost/schedule/performance snapshots of MDAPs. ([U.S. Department of War][8])


### Canada (NATO)

* **Treasury Board (Policy on Results + evaluation pages)** – policy & department evaluation publications; many defence-relevant. ([Government of Canada][9])
* **Office of the Auditor General (National Defence landing & report lists)** – performance/VfM audits (e.g., fighter replacement; supply chain; housing). ([oag-bvg.gc.ca][10])
* **Departmental evaluations/audits** – department sites host evaluation plans/reports (defence and related). ([Government of Canada][11])


### European / Multinational

* **NATO JALLC (Joint Analysis & Lessons Learned Centre)** – public “Explorer” publications and LL handbooks (broader LL/analysis outputs). ([jallc.nato.int][12])
* **European Defence Agency (EDA) – Defence Data** – comparable EU MS capability/spend datasets used in evaluations/benchmarking. ([eda.europa.eu][13])
* **European Court of Auditors (ECA)** – special reports on defence-related initiatives (EDF, military mobility, etc.). ([European Court of Auditors][14])

### France (NATO)

* **Cour des comptes (Ministère des Armées – mission “Défense”)** – recurrent audits on budget execution, organisation, bases, personnel. ([Cour des Comptes][15])


### Germany (NATO)

* **Bundesrechnungshof** – federal audit office (defence/Rüstungswesen audits periodically published). *(Portal varies by topic; use German keywords for targeted retrievals.)* ([Cour des Comptes][16])


### Netherlands (NATO)

* **Algemene Rekenkamer (Court of Audit) – Defence topic hub & publications** – defence capability, JSF costing, readiness, support to civil authorities. ([english.rekenkamer.nl][17])

### Sweden (NATO)

* **FOI (Swedish Defence Research Agency) – Reports** – technical/operational studies supporting evaluations. ([FOI][18])
* **Riksrevisionen (National Audit Office) – Defence/civil defence audits** – e.g., expansion of civil defence. ([riksrevisionen.se][19])

#### Norway (NATO)

* **Riksrevisjonen (Office of the Auditor General)** – defence acquisition performance audits (e.g., F-35 introduction). ([riksrevisjonen.no][20])

### Australia (ANZUS)

* **ANAO Major Projects Report (Defence)** – annual deep-dive on selected major acquisition projects, with methods and assurance. ([Australian National Audit Office][21])
* **Defence Test & Evaluation Strategy / One Defence Capability System Manual** – governance/TE frameworks; links to industry sessions. ([Defence][22])
* **DSTG publications** – scientific/technical reports supporting capability evaluations. ([dst.defence.gov.au][23])

### New Zealand (Five Eyes)

* **Office of the Auditor-General (Defence hub)** – multi-year performance audits & monitoring (e.g., Operation Respect). ([oag.parliament.nz][24])
* **NZDF public reporting** – annual reports; OIA-released evaluation/after-action artefacts (PAR/AAR, readiness metrics). ([NZDF][25])

### Japan (Indo-Pacific)

* **MOD White Papers / Strategy / Budget Materials** – outcome-oriented annexes and capability performance narratives (useful inputs to evaluations). ([Ministry of Defense Japan][26])


### Notes on scope

* In many jurisdictions, **“evaluation”** artefacts sit across **evaluation registries**, **audit offices**, **test & evaluation (OT\&E)** annuals, and **science/technology repositories**. The outlets above are where such evidence consistently lands in public.
* Some programme-level evaluations are **registered centrally** (e.g., UK Evaluation Registry) but published on the originating department pages; the registry points you to them. ([GOV.UK][1])
